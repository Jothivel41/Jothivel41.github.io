02 / How I work

Validate before you automate.

Years on a factory quality floor taught me one habit: never trust an output until you have checked the process behind it. Every project runs through the same four checkpoints.

  1. 1

    Understand the process

    Map who does what today, which systems hold the data and where the manual steps are.

  2. 2

    Validate the data

    Check fields, mappings and duplicates first, using the system's own valid values.

  3. 3

    Automate carefully

    Build in small steps, skip records that shouldn't change, and verify every update.

  4. 4

    Report and hand over

    Log what changed, summarise it for the team and document how it works.
